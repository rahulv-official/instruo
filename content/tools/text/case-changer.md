## Change text case instantly

Convert pasted text to lowercase, uppercase, sentence case, or title case. The result updates while you type and keeps line breaks intact.

## Common uses

Normalize copied text, repair accidental caps lock, prepare headings, or make user-entered text consistent before publishing.
