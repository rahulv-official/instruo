## Create a URL-friendly slug

Turn headings, product names, or article titles into lowercase, hyphen-separated text. Accents and unsupported punctuation are removed.

## Where slugs are used

Slugs make page URLs readable, such as `json-formatter` in `/tools/developer/json-formatter`.
