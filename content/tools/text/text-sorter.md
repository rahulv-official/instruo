## Sort lines of text

Sort non-empty lines alphabetically, by character length, or by numeric value. The sorted output updates with every change.

## Sorting behavior

Alphabetical sorting is locale-aware and handles numbers naturally. Numerical mode ignores lines that are not valid numbers.
