## Clean inconsistent spacing

Collapse repeated spaces, trim spaces around line breaks, and reduce three or more consecutive line breaks to one blank line.

## What stays intact

Paragraph boundaries remain readable. The tool does not rewrite words, punctuation, capitalization, or the order of your text.
