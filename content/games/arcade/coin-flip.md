## Rules

1. Press Flip coin.
2. Wait for the animated toss to finish.
3. Heads and tails have equal odds.

## A brief history

Coins have been used for binary decisions for thousands of years. Roman writers described similar lots and tosses, while modern coin tossing became a familiar way to settle a choice fairly.

## Privacy

Each result uses local browser randomness. No request or account is needed.
