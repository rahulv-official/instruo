## Rules

1. Turn over two hidden cards each move.
2. Matching cards stay face up.
3. Non-matching cards turn back after a short pause.
4. Find all eight pairs to finish the board.

## Controls

Select cards with pointer or touch. On desktop, move the selection with arrow keys and reveal with Enter or Space.

## A brief history

Memory matching games have long been played with ordinary cards. The familiar Concentration format brought the idea to television in 1958, then to popular home-game editions. This version keeps the central challenge: remember where each symbol appeared and find its partner with as few moves as possible.

Source: [The Strong National Museum of Play](https://www.museumofplay.org/blog/do-you-remember-these-memory-games/).

## Privacy

The shuffled deck, timer, and move count exist only in this browser tab. No account or network request is required.
