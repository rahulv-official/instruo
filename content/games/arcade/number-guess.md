## Rules

1. Enter a whole number between 1 and 100.
2. Each guess gives a higher or lower hint.
3. Solve the number in seven attempts.

## A brief history

Higher-or-lower guessing games are a classic form of paper-and-pencil play. Their small search space makes probability and binary-search ideas easy to feel through play.

## Privacy

The target and guesses stay in this browser tab.
