## Rules

1. Choose rock, paper, or scissors each round.
2. Rock beats scissors; scissors beat paper; paper beats rock.
3. Matching choices are a draw and do not change the score.
4. First side to five round wins takes the match.

## Controls

Select one of the three choices below the round display. Start a new match at any time.

## A brief history

Rock-paper-scissors belongs to a family of three-choice hand games associated with China and Japan, including the Japanese _janken_ tradition. Its circular win rule makes a fast, fair way to settle a choice: every hand beats one option and loses to another.

Source: [Rock-paper-scissors overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rock_paper_scissors).
