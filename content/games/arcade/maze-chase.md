## Rules

1. Collect every pellet in the maze to clear the level.
2. Power pellets turn the ghosts blue and make them vulnerable for a short time.
3. Touching a normal ghost costs one life.
4. Clear ten seeded mazes to finish the arcade run.

## Controls

Use arrow keys or WASD. Swipe across the board on touch screens. Press Space to start from the opening screen.

## A brief history

Maze-chase games became a defining arcade format in the early 1980s. Their simple loop—navigate a maze, collect dots, and outmaneuver enemies—made every turn readable while leaving room for mastery.

## Privacy

Mazes, scores, and progress stay in this browser. No account or network request is required.
