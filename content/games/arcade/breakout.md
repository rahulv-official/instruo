## Rules

1. Move the paddle under the ball.
2. Bounce the ball into every brick.
3. Missing the ball costs one of three lives.
4. Clear a pattern to advance. Every level changes the seeded layout and increases ball speed.

## Controls

Move with touch, pointer, arrow keys, or A/D. The paddle follows your pointer while the game is active.

## A brief history

Breakout was designed by Atari and released in 1976. Its single-paddle, brick-clearing loop turned Pong's rebound mechanic into a solo arcade challenge and inspired decades of block-breaking games.

## Privacy

The board, seed, score, level, and best score stay in this browser. No account or network request is required.
