## Rules

1. Tap the stage or press **Space** to make the bird flap upward.
2. Let the bird fall through the opening between each pair of pipes.
3. Passing a pipe earns one point.
4. Touching a pipe, the ceiling, or the ground ends the round.
5. After a round ends, choose **Try again** or press **Space** to start a fresh run.

## Controls

Click or tap the game stage, or use **Space**. **Enter** starts or retries a round. The game runs entirely in your browser and does not require an account.

## A brief history

Flappy Bird was created by Vietnamese developer Dong Nguyen and released for mobile in 2013. Its deliberately simple one-button control, narrow gaps, and high difficulty made it a widely shared score-chasing game. This version is an original local implementation for Instruo, with no external score service.

## Privacy

The game is generated and played locally. Your best score is stored only in this browser; Instruo does not upload scores or require an account.
