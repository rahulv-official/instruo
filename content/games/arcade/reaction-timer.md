## Rules

1. Start a round and wait for the panel to turn green.
2. Click as soon as the green signal appears.
3. Clicking during the waiting phase counts as a false start.
4. Your score is the elapsed time between the signal and your click. Lower is better.

The first click after a completed round starts another round. The best result is kept for this tab only.

## A brief history

Reaction-time tests became common in psychology and human-factors research during the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. Simple visual-response tests are still used to study attention, decision speed, and hand-eye coordination. This browser version keeps the experiment short and playful.

## Privacy

The signal delay, reaction time, and best score are calculated locally. No account or network request is required.
