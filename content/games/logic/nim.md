## Rules

1. Players alternate turns.
2. A turn removes one or more stones from exactly one pile.
3. The player taking the final stone wins.

This version uses three piles and supports a local two-player match or a solo match against a local computer opponent.

## A brief history

Nim is an ancient family of take-away games. The name became associated with the mathematical game in the nineteenth century, and its winning strategy helped popularize the study of impartial games.
