## Rules

1. Move the worker one square at a time through open floor.
2. Push crates onto the marked goals.
3. A crate can only be pushed, never pulled.
4. Complete all fifty levels to clear the warehouse.

## Controls

Use the arrow keys or WASD to move. Swipe or tap in a direction on touch screens. Press **Z** to undo one move and **R** to reset the current level.

## A brief history

Sokoban was created by Hiroyuki Imabayashi and first published in Japan in 1982. Its name combines the Japanese words for warehouse and keeper. The small ruleset—walk, push, and plan ahead—made it an enduring puzzle format across computers, consoles, and mobile devices.

## Privacy

Levels, moves, and undo history stay in this browser tab. No account or network request is required.
