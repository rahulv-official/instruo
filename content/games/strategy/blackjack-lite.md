## Rules

1. Start with two cards.
2. Hit to draw another card or stand to end your turn.
3. Going over 21 is a bust.
4. Dealer draws until reaching 17, then the closer score wins.

This is a friendly practice game with no betting, account, or multiplayer.

## A brief history

Blackjack grew from European twenty-one games and became popular in American casinos during the twentieth century. Its hit-or-stand decision makes it one of the most recognizable card games.
