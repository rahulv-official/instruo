## Rules

1. X takes the first turn, followed by O.
2. Select one empty square on each turn.
3. The first player to place three marks in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line wins.
4. If all nine squares are filled without a line, the round is a draw.

## Game modes

- Vs computer: you play X against a local minimax opponent that cannot be beaten.
- Two players: X and O take turns on the same device.

## A brief history

Three-in-a-row games have existed in many forms for centuries. Tic Tac Toe also holds a place in computing history: Alexander Douglas created OXO for Cambridge University's EDSAC computer in 1952, making it one of the earliest computer games.

Source: [Computer History Museum](https://www.computerhistory.org/timeline/1952/).
