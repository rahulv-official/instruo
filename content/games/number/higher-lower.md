## Rules

1. Read the current number.
2. Choose whether the next number is higher or lower.
3. Equal numbers end the round.
4. Continue until a prediction misses.

## A brief history

Higher-or-lower games appear in cards, quiz shows, and probability lessons. Their simple choice exposes how odds change as a range narrows.

## Privacy

Numbers and score remain local to this browser tab.
