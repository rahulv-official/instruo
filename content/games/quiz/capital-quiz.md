## Rules

1. Read the country shown.
2. Pick one capital from four choices.
3. The next question appears automatically.
4. Finish all four questions to see your score.

## A brief history

Geography quizzes became common in classrooms, newspapers, and radio programs during the twentieth century. Capital questions remain a compact way to practice map knowledge.

## Privacy

Questions and score stay in this browser tab.
