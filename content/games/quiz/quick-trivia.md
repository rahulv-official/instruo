## Rules

1. Answer one multiple-choice question at a time.
2. Your first answer is locked for that question.
3. See the correct answer immediately, then continue.
4. Finish all five questions to see your score.

## Controls

Select an answer, then choose **Next question**. There is no timer, so take your time.

## A brief history

Quiz games moved from newspapers and radio into television game shows, pub quizzes, board games, and later digital formats. Trivia competitions work because a short question creates a shared moment of recall, debate, or discovery. The 1980s success of _Trivial Pursuit_ helped make general-knowledge games a household staple.

Sources: [The Strong National Museum of Play](https://www.museumofplay.org/press-release/a-brief-history-of-game-shows/) and [Canadian Museum of History](https://www.historymuseum.ca/blog/five-amazing-facts-about-trivial-pursuit-the-most-successful-canadian-board-game-of-all-time).
