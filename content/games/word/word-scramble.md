## Rules

1. Read the shuffled letters shown in the game stage.
2. Enter the original word and submit your answer.
3. You have four attempts. Letter case does not matter.
4. Solve the word before the attempts run out to win.

The game uses a small, hand-picked list of common words. Every round is generated locally, and a new word never repeats the previous answer immediately.

## A brief history

Anagrams have been used as wordplay for centuries, from literary puzzles to newspaper games. Modern word scrambles became a staple of puzzle books and classroom activities because the rules are easy to learn while still rewarding pattern recognition and vocabulary.

## Privacy

The word list, scramble, and attempt count stay in this browser tab. No sign-up or network request is required.
