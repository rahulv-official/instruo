## Rules

1. Guess the hidden five-letter word in six attempts.
2. Every guess must be a valid word in the bundled dictionary.
3. Press Enter to submit. A submitted row cannot be changed.
4. Repeated letters are scored only as many times as they appear in the answer.

## Color guide

- Green means the letter is correct and in the correct position.
- Yellow means the letter is in the word but in a different position.
- Gray means the letter is not in the word.

## Controls

Use the on-screen keyboard or your physical keyboard. Backspace removes the last letter. Enter checks the current word.

## A brief history

Software engineer Josh Wardle created Wordle as a private game for his partner. He released it publicly in October 2021. Its simple daily format spread rapidly, and The New York Times acquired the game in January 2022.

Sources: [Hasbro and The New York Times announcement](https://corp.hasbro.com/node/34876/pdf) and [The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/media/2022/01/31/new-york-times-wordle/).

## Dictionary and privacy

Answers and accepted guesses come from a bundled local word list. The game needs no API request, account, or personal data.
