## Rules

1. Guess one letter at a time.
2. Every correct guess reveals every matching position in the word.
3. A wrong letter adds one part to the drawing.
4. Reveal every letter before six incorrect guesses to win.

## Controls

Use a physical keyboard or the on-screen letter keys. Repeated guesses do not count twice.

## A brief history

Hangman's precise origin is unclear, but a related word-guessing game appeared in Alice Bertha Gomme's 1894 collection _Traditional Games_. The format later became a common paper-and-pencil classroom and family game, then an early computer-game staple.

Sources: [Museums Victoria collection record](https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/2105242) and [Centre for Computing History](https://www.computinghistory.org.uk/det/18380/Hangman/).

## Privacy

Words and guesses remain in this browser tab. No account or network request is required.
